A small living room often gets treated with timidity: tiny furniture, stark white walls, and a fear of adding anything substantial. But in interior design, undersized furniture often accentuates the smallness of a space, leaving the room feeling like a dollhouse rather than a comfortable, lived-in sanctuary.

The goal is to create a living space that feels both generous and intimate. By understanding proportion, sightlines, and cohesive color palettes, you can craft a living room that welcomes long evenings with books, casual entertaining, and daily calm.

---

## 1. Scale Up the Rug

The most common misconception in small spaces is that a small room needs a small rug. A rug that floats only beneath the coffee table segments the floor, making the perimeter feel boxed in.

Instead, select a rug that extends almost to the walls, leaving an 8-to-12-inch border of exposed floor. Placing the front legs of your sofa and accent chairs directly on the rug unifies the seating zone into a single expansive statement.

## 2. Embrace Low-Profile, Tailored Seating

Look for sofas with clean track arms, tight backs, and exposed legs. Bulky rolled arms can steal up to 10 inches of room width on either side without providing any extra seating area. A tailored, bench-cushioned linen sofa keeps sightlines open and looks impeccably composed.

## 3. Hang Drapery High and Wide

Mount your curtain rod just two to three inches below the ceiling moulding—not directly above the window frame. Extend the rod 8 to 12 inches past the window casing on each side. 

When the drapery is open, the fabric stacks against the wall rather than blocking the glass, making the windows appear taller and maximizing natural daylight.

> *"Always hang drapery ceiling-high. It draws the gaze upward and tricks the brain into registering the vertical volume of the room."*

## 4. Float an Armchair Opposite the Sofa

Rather than squeezing an L-shaped sectional that locks your traffic flow into a corner, pair a three-seater sofa with an airy accent chair with an open wood frame. This allows conversation to flow while keeping the entrance to the room unobstructed.

## 5. Use Wall-Mounted Lighting Instead of Floor Lamps

Floor lamps consume footprint with their bases. Hardwired or plug-in adjustable swing-arm sconces mounted above the sofa provide reading light while keeping the floor completely clear for walking paths.
